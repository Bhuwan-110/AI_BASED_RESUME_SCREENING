TERMS & Architectural Rationale
1. Term Frequency (TF-IDF) Extracts distinctive vocabulary from the job vacancy and the candidate's resume, dampening ubiquitous grammatical noise words using standard English stop-word filtering.
2. Cosine Similarity Angle Measures the directional alignment of the high-dimensional feature vectors. Because it normalizes by vector magnitude, conciseness is never penalized.
3. Privacy & Reliability All NLP processing runs locally on the host server without transmitting candidate personal data to external third-party LLMs or commercial APIs.
