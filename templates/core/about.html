About Our Institution

About GURUKUL

GURUKUL is an online teaching, learning, and academic mentorship platform committed to structured curriculum delivery, civil service preparation, and foundational education in Nepal.

Our Background

Accessible Quality Education for Every Learner

Named after the ancient tradition of dedicated, holistic learning, GURUKUL was envisioned as an academic bridge connecting experienced educators, subject specialists, and civil servants with students across Nepal.

Whether a candidate is preparing for the highly competitive Public Service Commission (Loksewa Aayog) examinations, a secondary student seeking conceptual clarity for the Secondary Education Examination (SEE), or a high schooler aiming for higher college milestones, GURUKUL provides structured, honest, and high-standard academic guidance.

To democratize quality education across Nepal by offering accessible, syllabus-focused instruction and compassionate academic mentoring.

To be the most trusted, reliable educational and preparatory ecosystem empowering students to achieve their academic and civil service goals.

  • Clarity over Rote Learning: Emphasis on conceptual clarity and problem-solving.
  • Transparency: Honest course outlines, real faculty profiles, and ethical decision support.

Academic Scope

Four Fundamental Pillars

How GURUKUL supports students throughout their educational and career journey.

1. Loksewa Preparation
Public Service Commission (PSC) Nepal

Civil service recruitment in Nepal demands deep familiarity with constitutional frameworks, current affairs, public administration, and subjective answer-writing techniques. GURUKUL provides comprehensive test preparation series, past question analyses, and mock exam simulations.

2. School Education (SEE)
Classes 8, 9 & 10 Foundation

Secondary education forms the backbone of a student’s future. Our courses focus on Compulsory Mathematics, Optional Mathematics, Science, and English, using simplified derivations and step-by-step problem sets to build genuine subject mastery.

3. College Education (+2)
Higher Secondary & University Readiness

For Grade 11 and 12 students across Science and Management streams, GURUKUL provides subject-matter tutorials in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Accountancy, and Economics, reinforcing syllabus mastery for NEB board evaluations.

4. Academic Mentorship
Individual Study Strategy & Guidance

Education requires personal encouragement. Our mentorship wing links students directly with senior instructors and advisors to discuss study plans, revision methods, stress management, and interview preparation.
