Our Culture

Why Teach & Mentor with GURUKUL

Nationwide Reach

Guide ambitious students across all seven provinces of Nepal preparing for civil service examinations and secondary school milestones.

Flexible Formats

Transparent Recruitment

Evaluation Workflow

Four-Stage Recruitment Framework

Combining objective AI decision support with expert human evaluators.

1
Application & CV Upload

Candidate submits resume in PDF format with education and teaching details.

2
AI Semantic Screening

Text extraction and TF-IDF cosine similarity calculate advisory match score against role requirements.

3
Human Administrator Review

Academic coordinators review candidate profiles and verify pedagogical qualifications.

4
Interview & Onboarding

Shortlisted candidates are invited for classroom demonstration and session planning.
