About GURUKUL

Comprehensive Educational Programs

We bridge the gap between classroom instruction and competitive academic readiness through focused educational tracks.

Loksewa Preparation

Tailored curriculum coverage for Civil Service Commission examinations in Nepal, including General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Constitution, and Public Administration with regular practice drills.

School Education

Reinforcing core competencies for secondary school learners (Classes 8 through 10) in Compulsory Mathematics, Science, and English to build solid conceptual understanding for SEE board success.

College Education

Supporting +2 Science, Management, and Humanities students through deep-dive lectures in Physics, Chemistry, Accountancy, and Economics, alongside university entrance preparation.

Academic Mentorship

Individualized guidance where mentors evaluate student study strategies, answer writing techniques, time management, and mock interview readiness to build confidence.
